# Why PowerShield License?

Most permissive licenses focus primarily on freedom of use.
However, they do not restrict harmful or unethical usage of software.

PowerShield License was created to address this gap.

## Problems PSL tries to solve

- Lack of ethical boundaries in permissive licenses
- Difficulty for authors to express usage preferences
- Increasing misuse of open software in harmful systems
- Need for readable, simple licensing with intent

## What PSL is

- A permissive source-available license
- A license with attribution requirements
- A license with ethical restrictions
- A readable alternative to complex legal frameworks

## What PSL is not

- Not a traditional OSI open source license
- Not a copyleft license
- Not a proprietary license

## Summary

PSL is designed for developers who want freedom with defined responsibility.
